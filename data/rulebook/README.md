# Policy Rulebook

24 clauses across 8 categories.

## STRUCTURING

- **STRUCT-001** - Cash transaction reporting threshold and sub-threshold structuring
- **STRUCT-002** - Series of integrally connected cash transactions
- **STRUCT-003** - Sudden surge in account turnover

## KYC_CDD

- **KYC-001** - Customer due diligence and risk categorisation
- **KYC-002** - Inconsistency between declared profile and observed activity
- **KYC-003** - Periodic updation and change in customer profile

## SANCTIONS

- **SANC-001** - Designated persons and entities under Section 51A
- **SANC-002** - Name screening, aliases and match resolution
- **SANC-003** - Reporting obligations on a positive designation match

## NETWORK

- **NET-001** - Beneficial ownership and control
- **NET-002** - Accounts linked by shared operating identifiers
- **NET-003** - Politically exposed persons and close associates

## DOCUMENT_FORGERY

- **DOC-001** - Officially valid documents for identity and address
- **DOC-002** - Indications of tampering or fabrication in submitted documents
- **DOC-003** - Expired or invalid identity documents

## CROSS_BORDER

- **XB-001** - Originator and beneficiary information on cross-border transfers
- **XB-002** - Liberalised Remittance Scheme limits and permissible purposes
- **XB-003** - Remittances involving higher-risk jurisdictions and concentrated counterparties
- **XB-004** - Reporting of cross-border wire transfers above five lakh rupees
- **XB-005** - Round-tripping of funds through overseas entities

## MONEY_MULE

- **MULE-001** - Operation of bank accounts and money mules
- **MULE-002** - Pass-through of funds from unrelated remitters: suspected money-mule transactions

## SUSPICIOUS_TRANSACTION

- **STR-001** - Suspicious transactions on any payment channel
- **STR-002** - Domestic non-cash transfers: no threshold report, examined by pattern
